# Generated-name vocabulary: english-v1

A curated subset of **EFF's Large Wordlist for Passphrases**, created by the
Electronic Frontier Foundation, Joseph Bonneau, 2016.

Changes: selected common short ASCII nature/craft words, removed dice codes and
all unselected words, sorted the subset and retained it as a versioned TypeScript
asset. Generated names capitalize and concatenate three to five independent
selections. Every selected word was checked against the downloaded source.
No endorsement by EFF or its authors is implied.

The subset excludes imported proper names, distinctive brands, profanity, slurs,
sexual/violent terms and identified homophones; review considered ordinary public
name combinations rather than password suitability. Vocabulary is product content,
not a promise that all future cultural interpretations of all combinations are
harmless. Changes require explicit versioning, code review and output tests; never
fetch or silently replace the vocabulary at runtime. Names are not credentials.
